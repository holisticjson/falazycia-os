# Email Drafter — Input Template

Use this template to give Claude the context it needs to draft your email. Fill in as much or as little as you want — the more detail, the better the draft.

## Required

- **To:** [Name and role/relationship — e.g. "Sarah, my manager" or "Cold lead from LinkedIn"]
- **Purpose:** [What is this email about?]
- **Goal:** [What do you want the reader to do after reading?]

## Optional (improves quality)

- **Tone:** [Formal / Professional / Casual / Friendly / Urgent]
- **Key points to include:** [Any specific details, data, or arguments]
- **Things to avoid:** [Topics, phrases, or tones to stay away from]
- **Context:** [Any backstory the reader already knows]
- **Your name:** [For the sign-off]

## Example filled in

- **To:** David Chen, VP of Marketing at Acme Corp (cold outreach)
- **Purpose:** Introduce our analytics platform and get a demo booked
- **Goal:** Get him to reply and agree to a 15-minute call
- **Tone:** Professional but not stiff
- **Key points:** We helped a competitor cut reporting time by 60%. Our platform integrates with their existing stack.
- **Things to avoid:** Don't mention the competitor by name. Don't oversell.
- **Your name:** Alex
